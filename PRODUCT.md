# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind CSS, and TypeScript as explicitly requested.

## Users

Individuals, professionals, businesses, and small and medium-sized businesses who
need help learning, choosing, configuring, or making better use of technology.

## Product Purpose

Gravity Digital provides technology training, IT and equipment-purchasing advice,
process automation, and remote technical support. The website should help
visitors understand these services and decide which kind of support fits their
needs.

## Positioning

Gravity Digital explains the solutions it implements and helps clients learn to
use them, rather than treating technical work as a black box.

## Operating Context

Initial coverage is Formosa, Argentina. Services are primarily remote, with
in-person work coordinated when appropriate. Remote services may be provided to
other regions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Training is tailored to a person's or team's level and goals and may cover
  digital tools, AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and n8n.
- Technology advice covers computers, components, peripherals, phones, and
  network equipment (including routers, switches, racks, and servers), cloud
  storage, automation, and generative AI, based on needs and available budget.
- Automation connects tools and simplifies repetitive workflows; implementations
  are explained to clients.
- Remote technical support covers diagnosis, configuration, software issues, and
  maintenance.
- There is no backend, database, analytics, or contact form for this website.
  The business's WhatsApp, email, and Instagram channels are confirmed and
  centralized in `src/lib/site-config.ts`; calls can use Zoom, Google Meet, or
  the client's preferred tool.
- Do not fabricate prices, promotions, customers, testimonials, statistics, or
  certifications.

## Brand Commitments

- The brand name is Gravity Digital.
- The slogan is “Visión Técnica, apoyo informático donde sea”.
- The supplied logo is a black mark on a transparent background.
- The established identity is monochrome, based on black and white.
- Website copy should use clear, neutral Spanish.

## Product Principles

- Tailor recommendations to the client's actual needs and budget.
- Make technology understandable and useful to clients.
- Explain the work and how implemented solutions operate.
- Offer practical support to both individuals and organizations.
