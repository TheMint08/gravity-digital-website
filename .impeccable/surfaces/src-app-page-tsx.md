# Gravity Digital homepage

## Scope and visitor mode

New Spanish-language commercial landing page. Persuade visitors to understand
the services and choose a suitable next step.

## Visitor and constraints

Individuals, professionals, businesses, and SMEs in Formosa and remote regions
need help learning, choosing, configuring, or using technology. The page must
explain four real services, compare processes without invented outcomes, and
show how work is delivered. Use the supplied monochrome brand mark. No official
contact channel, price, promotion, customer proof, statistic, or certification
is confirmed.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Gravity Digital makes technology understandable by showing the
connections behind it. Refuse the generic equal-card SaaS page and claims of
measured productivity.

**OWN-WORLD:** Black, white, and cool paper gray hold the page. A continuous
cobalt trace carries the visual system of an explained circuit. Use cobalt
`#2456D6`, signal red `#B6453F` only for friction, ink `#111216`, paper
`#F5F6F8`, and white `#FFFFFF`. Bricolage Grotesque gives headlines a distinct
voice; Source Sans 3 keeps service explanations open and readable. The supplied
black logo stays on a light surface.

**STORY:** Visitors recognize a practical technology need, see how training,
purchase advice, automation, and support address it, then understand the clear
working process and coverage. Primary action: explore services. Contact
channels remain explicitly pending; no form or dead link is shown.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A light, compact navigation bar places the black mark at
left and working in-page links at right. Below it, one full-width ink field
holds a large left-aligned promise, concise explanation, and cobalt service
anchor; an original circuit-orbit drawing occupies the right, with ample
negative space and no metric or product mockup.

**FORM:** Assigned direction 3: hand-bent neon circuit, source
`magnetism-electricity-plasma-hand-bent-neon-circuit`, translated into an
explained technical pathway rather than a neon sign. Seed reference:
`seed:87124adf0c3ee21b`. Risk: a luminous circuit can overpower the quiet
monochrome brand; confine its glow to the hero and workflow paths.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved decisions

Official contact channels, final pricing, and launch promotions remain
unconfirmed and must be supplied by the business before they are published.
